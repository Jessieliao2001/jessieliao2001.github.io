About

I am a Predoctoral Research Professional in the Macro Finance Research Program at the University of Chicago, working with Professor Lars Peter Hansen.

My research interests span macro-finance modeling, macroeconomics, economic growth, and political economy. Away from research, I am a photographer who enjoys capturing urban landscapes and everyday moments.
